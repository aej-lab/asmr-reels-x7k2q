ASMR Explains Uploader

ASMR Explains Uploader is a small private automation tool used by the owner of the ASMR Explains YouTube channel (@asmr_explains). It uploads the channel owner's own short science explainer videos to that same channel through the YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other users.
